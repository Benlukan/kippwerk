In short

KIPPWERK does not collect any personal data by itself. We do not run our own server, there is no KIPPWERK account and no analytics; the game works offline.

Exception – leaderboards and cloud save: Only if you sign in to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center in the app are your results and progress sent to that service (see below).

Exception – advertising: The Android and iOS app shows ads via Google AdMob – optional videos for a “rescue” and occasionally a full-screen ad between two rounds. Google processes data for this (see below). The web version contains no ads.

Data stored locally

The following is stored on your device – and only there:

  • your leaderboard (Endless per board size and daily game) and your stage progress,
  • your daily game results,
  • your player name and your randomly generated player ID,
  • your settings (e.g. language, sound, vibration, colors),
  • your extra lives, rewards and leaderboard badges as well as the leaderboard rank last seen (for the rewards).

Storing this data in your device's local storage is strictly necessary for the game (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG) and therefore requires no consent. The data does not leave your device – except for the optional cloud save and leaderboards (see below). You can delete it at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app, or by removing the site data in your browser for the web version.

Streak and reminder

Your daily streak, streak shields, badges and chosen theme are stored locally on your device only. The optional evening reminder in the app is a local notification: it is scheduled on your device, no data is transmitted and no push service is used. You can turn it off in the app settings or in your device's notification settings.

Sharing

When you share your result, a text (score and emoji history) is copied to the clipboard or handed to your device's share menu. You decide where to send it; KIPPWERK does not send anything to us.

Global leaderboards: Google Play Games / Apple Game Center (optional, app only)

In the app you can submit your results to the global leaderboards of Google Play Games (Android) or Apple Game Center (iPhone). To do so, you sign in to that service with your Google or Apple account. KIPPWERK itself runs no server for this and receives no account or profile data.

What data: If the service is signed in on your device, KIPPWERK submits your best Endless results per board size and your counted daily game (score). For the top 5 in the menu and the leaderboard rewards, the app retrieves the leaderboard (player names, scores, ranks) from the provider; this data is only displayed and not sent to us. The provider links your scores to your player profile (player name/gamer tag, avatar) and shows them to other players – depending on your visibility settings in your Play Games or Game Center profile.

Legal basis: your consent by signing in (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without signing in, no results are transmitted; the game works fully without it.

Providers: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (privacy policy) and Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Cork, Ireland (privacy policy). Data may be transferred to the USA; both companies are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Cloud save: If you are signed in, KIPPWERK stores your progress (stages and stars, records, daily game results, streak, player name, game settings and the app's random player ID) as a “saved game” in your Google Play Games or Game Center/iCloud account so you can continue on your other devices. This data is visible only to you, not to other players.

Withdrawal and deletion: You can sign out and manage or delete your profile and game data in the Play Games app or in the iPhone settings under “Game Center”.

Advertising in the app (Google AdMob)

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

What data: the device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA, only with your permission), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information (e.g. model, operating system, language), interactions with ads and diagnostic data.

Purpose: serving and billing ads, measuring reach, preventing fraud and – only with consent – personalized advertising.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). On first launch, a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) asks whether and for what purposes data may be used. Without consent, Google shows at most limited, non-personalized ads. On iOS, Apple additionally asks whether the app may track you across apps.

Withdrawal: at any time in the game settings via “Privacy settings (ads)”, in your device settings (reset/delete advertising ID or “Tracking” on iOS) or by uninstalling the app.

Third country: Google may transfer data to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the EU Commission).

Web version (GitHub Pages)

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address to deliver the page and keep the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details: GitHub privacy statement. Fonts and all game files are loaded from this site, not from third parties.

App stores

When you download the app from Google Play or the Apple App Store, the privacy terms of the respective store operator apply. We have no influence over the data collected there.

Your rights

We do not store any personal data about you on our own servers. Requests about leaderboard and saved-game data go to Google or Apple, requests about advertising data to Google; we are happy to help by email. You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony (Germany).

Children

KIPPWERK is aimed at teenagers aged 13 and over and adults. The app is not specifically intended for children.
